# The first levels, played through (Phase 7 round 9 x1: level 1; round 10 y1 and round 11 z1: level 2; round 11 z3: levels 3 and 4)

Level 1 of Turok 2, the Port of Adia, played from a new game to its end on the native port
(Linux, headless): every sub-level from 51 to 59, both mission objectives (the three distress
beacons and the four children), the two portal switches, the exit warp 7100 to the Harbor Totem
(level 109), the totem defense, and the warp back to the hub (level 60). The script is
reproducible: one `--input @FILE` and one `T2_TELEPORT` list, generated by
`tools/port/playthrough.py`. Teleports only put the player next to each progression point,
facing it. From there the game's own logic does each step: the pickup, the switch, the cage
door, the child, the beacon, the objective counters and messages, and the warp.

with a key byte (0x0F) decrements that link group, and the group's records fire when it reaches 0
("all distress beacons activated. mission complete.").

## 4. The totem defense (level 109)

`TotemDefense_InitFight`/`_Init` start a 120 s defense (`m_TotemMissionDuration`): kill 8
enemies (`m_EnemiesToKillToSaveTotem`) before the time runs out. Four generators (actors 14, 19,
20, 21, type 1604) on the walls start when their gates (actors 1-4) open, at most 3 spawns alive
at a time (`CInstancePool__Spawn`). The state log prints a `totem:` line (state, time left,
kills, the totem's health).

- Before X1-7 the spawns never moved: no kills, the time ran out, `TotemDefense_Fail`, and after
  75 frames `TotemDefense_Update` warped to 1999: the hub.
- After X1-7/X1-8 the enemies come over the walls, attack the totem and Turok and die: with the
  pistol (`T2_WEAPON=4`), `T2_FACE_NEAREST=4000` and a tap of Z every 15 frames, 6 of 8 kills in
  120 s (the time runs out at 6/8: `TotemDefense_Fail`, then the hub); with the plasma rifle
  (`T2_WEAPON=10`): from `T2_WARP_TO=-110` 10/8 kills, saved with 23 s left; in the full
  scripted run (`--fight 10`) 8/8 with 3 s left (Turok down to 36 health). Then
  `TotemDefense_Complete` ("MISSION COMPLETE", the totem's rest animation,
  `m_bLevel1TotemFlags`, cheat bit 0x8000), the helpers stop, and 90 frames later the warp to 1999,
  the hub.
- Attract demo 3 plays in this level (20,946-22,148 of the boot loop): its Raptoids now come in and
  hit Turok too (health 99 at 21,600); the boot loop's mode switches are unchanged (25,778 the
  title again).

How the records work (`CScene__GetInstanceEventRecords`, `CScene__GetRegionEventRecords`): the
first table of a level's links block is sorted by the **owning actor index** (`levels.py`'s
`id`): an actor's records fire when it fires its triggers (mask 1: its death, 0x4000: a door
opening, 0x80000: an action object "go"); the second table is sorted by **region info index**:
the player entering a region of that info fires them (mask 1, `CPlayer` move,
func_0024C47C). The record's key byte is the link group it counts down (the doors that open
after N deaths). The graveyards wake their zombies by region (133: info 4 near door 27, info 7
the sister's hall; 135: info 8 opens door 59, info 9 "graveyard located." and the generators).

### 6.4 New debug switches (DEBUG-ONLY, port/game/t2debug.c)

- `T2_AIM_AT=F1-F2:x,y,z[;...]`: from F1 to F2 turn the player (yaw and camera pitch) at a
  point: the soul gates (not actors the aim assists know).
- `T2_AIM_ENEMY=F1-F2[,R][;...]`: `T2_AIM_NODE=-1` (the nearest live actor, yaw and pitch) only in
  those windows, so the walks between fights keep their facing. The flying sisters need the pitch.
- `T2_TELEPORT=F:x,y,z[,rotY]!`: a `!` also fires the region's "entered" triggers
  (`CGameRegion__FireTriggers(region, 1)`, what the player's move does): the graveyards' zombies
  only wake when the player walks into their halls.
- `T2_EVENT_LOG`: `objectives A/B` is now the counters of the current level's hub (`T2Port_HubLevelOf`).
- Round 11 (z1): `T2_AIM_ENEMY=F1-F2,R,A`: level actor A first (the nearest live actor when A is
  dead or out of range; `T2_AIM_NODE=actor:N[,R]` underneath); `T2_FACE_PITCH=1`: `T2_FACE_NEAREST`
  also sets the camera pitch (the portable atan2f of t2bosslog.c).

**Fixed: `Primagen_EnterMode24`** (func_004077AC, asm_diff 0). This is the recurring N64-offset
view bug. The function views `LevelScriptState` from its `timer` with N64 deltas: 0x50 for
`dest.y`, 0x5C for `home.y`, -0xC for `bossState`. On the host the script blocks between them hold
pointers, so the write `dest.y = home.y` (or 0) landed on another field. Mode 24 (stage 2's landing
after bossState 6, Primagen_UpdateMode24) waits until he reaches `dest.y`. He lands on the floor
at y 0 while `dest.y` still held the lair's 588.8, so the fight hung in mode 24 for good. z2's
fresh recipe never takes that branch. The host body sets the members. The module-2 twin
`Primagen_EnterMode24_2` (func_004407AC, raw 0x1FC/0xD4 actor offsets and the same deltas) is only
in the compile-only library.
