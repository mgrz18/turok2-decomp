# The first level, played through (Phase 7 round 9, part x1)

Level 1 of Turok 2, the Port of Adia, played from a new game to its end on the native port
(Linux, headless): every sub-level from 51 to 59, both mission objectives (the three distress
beacons and the four children), the two portal switches, the exit warp 7100 to the Harbor Totem
(level 109), the totem defense, and the warp back to the hub (level 60). The script is
reproducible: one `--input @FILE` and one `T2_TELEPORT` list, generated by
`tools/port/playthrough.py`. Teleports only put the player next to each progression point,
facing it. From there the game's own logic does each step: the pickup, the switch, the cage
door, the child, the beacon, the objective counters and messages, and the warp.

**The objectives** (`gLevelMessages` 1 to 4; the link-group types table, cart 19, maps groups to
objective numbers: group 31 is objective 2, group 33 is objective 3, and
`Mission_LevelObjectivesDone(1)` wants both counts at 0):

- 3 distress beacons (action objects 278, intelligence 567): 51 actor 17 at (1690, -307, 0), 53
  actor 56 at (256, 10, 7834), 53 actor 57 at (-2150, 625, 3674). Each needs a **power cell**
  (pickup id 4200; `Action_CheckRequiredItem` shows "power cell required."): 51 (2304, 0, 0), 53
  (-3989, 0, 4667) and (1536, 0, 6973).
- 4 children (object 327) in cages (object 429), each cage opened by a switch (object 167, link
  id = the switch's instance index): 52 switch 18 / child 20, 53 switch 44 / cage 100 / child 60,
  54 switch 13 / cage 36 / child 22, 57 switch 25 / cage 46 / child 35.
- Portals (object 388, "portal activated."): 55 switch 14 -> portal 21 (warp 1001, the
  talisman), 59 switch 52 -> portal 74 (warp 1003). Not needed for the exit.
- The exit: `CPlayer__Warp` redirects 7100 to the hub (1000, "level not complete") while the
  objectives are open; once they are done, to the totem (109) while `m_bLevel1TotemFlags` is 0,
  else to 1999 (the hub).

`T2_EVENT_LOG=1` (new) prints every level event `LevelEvent_Fire` fires: the frame, the
event's actor and level, its kind/handler, the message, and the two objective counters
(`objectives B/C`). Level events fire on trigger masks (`GameObject_FireTriggers`): 0x2 is a
touch, 0x80000 an action object going to "go" (`Action_GoEnter`), 0x4000 a door/gate. A record
with a key byte (0x0F) decrements that link group, and the group's records fire when it reaches 0
("all distress beacons activated. mission complete.").

## 2. The run

```sh
cd OUT
python3 ../tools/port/playthrough.py . --fight 10   # playthrough.txt and teleport.env (exports)
. ./teleport.env
SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen T2_EVENT_LOG=1 T2_STATE_LOG=100 T2_PICKUP_LOG=1 \
    T2_SHOT=shot.ppm T2_SHOT_EVERY=100 T2_SHOT_FROM=13000 \
    build/app/turok2 --archive turok2.o2r --rom ../baserom.us.z64 --game --draw --pak-blank \
    --frames 27000 --input @playthrough.txt
```

To also **win the totem defense**: `python3 ../tools/port/playthrough.py . --fight 10` (fire
taps from the totem's arrival; `teleport.env` then also sets `T2_GIVE_WEAPONS=1 T2_WEAPON=10`,
the plasma rifle, and `T2_FACE_NEAREST=4000@109`, the aim assist in level 109 only; new:
`T2_FACE_NEAREST=R@LEVEL`, and it now also aims at pooled instances, the generators' spawns),
and `--frames 27000`. `T2_TELEPORT` entries may carry a facing:
`F:x,y,z,rotY` (new; the player walks along (-sin rotY, -cos rotY) in x/z).
`tools/port/approach.py LEVELS LEVEL --warp ID` (or `--point x,y,z`) prints such an entry a few
regions from a warp region or a point, facing it (`--water` also walks through water regions).

## 4. The totem defense (level 109)

`TotemDefense_InitFight`/`_Init` start a 120 s defense (`m_TotemMissionDuration`): kill 8
enemies (`m_EnemiesToKillToSaveTotem`) before the time runs out. Four generators (actors 14, 19,
20, 21, type 1604) on the walls start when their gates (actors 1-4) open, at most 3 spawns alive
at a time (`CInstancePool__Spawn`). The state log prints a `totem:` line (state, time left,
kills, the totem's health).

- Before X1-7 the spawns never moved: no kills, the time ran out, `TotemDefense_Fail`, and after
  75 frames `TotemDefense_Update` warped to 1999: the hub.
- After X1-7/X1-8 the enemies come over the walls, attack the totem and Turok and die: with the
  pistol (`T2_WEAPON=4`), `T2_FACE_NEAREST=4000` and a tap of Z every 15 frames, 6 of 8 kills in
  120 s (the time runs out at 6/8: `TotemDefense_Fail`, then the hub); with the plasma rifle
  (`T2_WEAPON=10`): from `T2_WARP_TO=-110` 10/8 kills, saved with 23 s left; in the full
  scripted run (`--fight 10`) 8/8 with 3 s left (Turok down to 36 health). Then
  `TotemDefense_Complete` ("MISSION COMPLETE", the totem's rest animation,
  `m_bLevel1TotemFlags`, cheat bit 0x8000), the helpers stop, and 90 frames later the warp to 1999,
  the hub.
- Attract demo 3 plays in this level (20,946-22,148 of the boot loop): its Raptoids now come in and
  hit Turok too (health 99 at 21,600); the boot loop's mode switches are unchanged (25,778 the
  title again).
